# ⚡ Velocity & Speed Analysis

This document provides context on how velocity is assessed and tracked.

---

## 🧭 Why Velocity, Not Deadlines?

Traditional deadlines in self-paced learning lead to:
- Skimming complex topics just to "check the box".
- Anxiety when personal life or work interrupts study.
- Copy-pasting AI solutions without understanding.

**Velocity tracking**, on the other hand, measures **throughput and learning momentum**:
1. **Cycle Time**: How many days between picking up a task and getting PR approval.
2. **Effort Efficiency**: How many hours of focused coding are needed to master a concept.
3. **Friction Analysis**: High difficulty scores (4 or 5) indicate topics where the mentor should provide supplementary diagrams, pairing sessions, or simplified analogies.
